# SupplyCompare — Platform Administrator Guide

As a platform administrator, you maintain system governance, oversee account verification, and ensure operational integrity.

---

## 1. Accessing Administration
Log in with administrator credentials (e.g. `admin@supplycompare.local`). The administrator console is located at `/admin`.

## 2. User Governance & Security Controls
* **Account Review**: View all registered shopkeepers, suppliers, and administrative staff.
* **Status Enforcement**: Activate or suspend accounts in real-time. Suspended accounts are immediately prevented from generating quotations, accessing comparison data, or submitting purchase orders.
* **Role Auditing**: Inspect user roles and registered shop names.

## 3. Product Catalog Governance
* Review master SKU entries across all retail categories.
* Ensure unit conversion ratios (e.g. cartons to packets, quintals to kg) remain accurate across wholesale distributors.
