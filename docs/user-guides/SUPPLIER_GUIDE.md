# SupplyCompare — Supplier Portal User Guide

As a wholesale supplier or distributor on SupplyCompare, you can manage orders received from small retail clients and update product quotation terms.

---

## 1. Accessing the Supplier Portal
Log in with your supplier credentials (e.g. `supplier@supplycompare.local`). The portal is located at `/supplier-portal`.

## 2. Order Fulfillment Workflow
1. **Review Inbound Orders**: View orders submitted by retail shops with full line items, delivery requirements, and total payment amounts.
2. **Confirm Acceptance**: When your inventory is ready for allocation, click **Confirm Order**.
3. **Dispatch & Logistics**: Once items leave your warehouse with your transport vehicle or courier, click **Dispatch & Ship**.
4. **Delivery Receipt**: The retailer verifies the physical counts on arrival and records good vs damaged packaging. Once accepted, the order moves to **Fulfilled**.

## 3. Maintaining Quotations
* Review active quotation pricing, MOQ thresholds, and expiration dates.
* Update lead times and volume discounts to ensure small retailers receive competitive offers during comparison simulations.
