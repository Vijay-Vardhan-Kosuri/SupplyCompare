# Phase 1233d: Pricing Intelligence

## Summary

analyze market price variance and supplier competitiveness to surface the best procurement decisions in real time.

## Changes

- map demand, pricing, and supplier health signals into a single operating view

This initiative keeps the supply ecosystem stable, measurable, and resilient as purchasing volume and supplier complexity continue to rise.
