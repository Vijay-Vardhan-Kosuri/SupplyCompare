# Phase 1133d: Demand Forecasting

## Summary

improve inventory planning by combining seasonal signals, previous demand curves, and supplier lead-time variability.

## Changes

- map demand, pricing, and supplier health signals into a single operating view
- instrument coverage checks, latency budgets, and anomaly thresholds for the platform
- validate replenishment and sourcing assumptions against live operating data
- document rollout guidance and rollback checkpoints for cross-functional teams

## Why this matters

This initiative keeps the supply ecosystem stable, measurable, and resilient as purchasing volume and supplier complexity continue to rise.
